Private Measure

On-device AR measuring for objects, furniture, and rooms. Nothing leaves your phone.

Privacy: No accounts, ads, analytics, or network calls after load. Measurements stay in this browser’s local storage on your phone only. Open in Incognito if you also want no Chrome history of this page.

WebXR AR not available on this browser/device.

Use Chrome on an Android phone that supports ARCore. Then return here and tap Start AR.

Accuracy tips

  • Good light and textured floors/walls work best (rugs, wood grain, tile).
  • Move the phone slowly to scan the surface before placing points.
  • Expect roughly ±1 inch over a few feet; blank walls and dim light are worse.
  • For rooms, walk the floor corners in order around the perimeter.

How to use

Line

Tap two points. Shows length between them. Tap again to start a new line, or Undo/Reset.

Furniture / Box

1) Tap two corners of the front edge (width). 2) Tap a point for depth. 3) Tap a point up for height (or on the top surface). Shows W × D × H.

Room

Tap floor corners in order around the room. Shows each wall, perimeter, and floor area (sq ft or m²). Optionally tap a wall/ceiling point for height after closing the floor polygon (tap near the first corner to close).

Units

Toggle ft/in or metric anytime. Saved items keep the unit used when saved.

Saved

Stored only in this browser on this phone (localStorage). Clearing site data or using Incognito will remove them.
